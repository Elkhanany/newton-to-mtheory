The Long Argument

Eight arguments that never finished — read in pairs, across the centuries that kept reopening them.

Eight questions opened early and never closed. Each thread follows one of them forward through everyone who took it up, so an argument reads as a conversation across centuries rather than a list of positions — and every thinker on the timeline has a study of his own: the history happening around him, how his books came to be written, what each one argues, and what survived him.
